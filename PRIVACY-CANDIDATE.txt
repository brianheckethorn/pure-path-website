Limits:
Removing direct identifiers does not make a health report fully deidentified.
Age and health answers may identify someone. A numeric identifier deliberately
entered in a numeric health field cannot be distinguished automatically from a
measurement. Start over cannot delete saved, printed or shared copies. Manual
sharing leaves the page through a user-selected application; delivery is not
verified. The approximate health-age estimate remains a rough, unvalidated
educational estimate, not biological age or a diagnosis.
